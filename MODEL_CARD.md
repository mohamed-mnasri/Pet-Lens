# Model card: PetLens breed suggester

_Generated by `make cards` from the run the app serves. Do not edit by hand._

## Intended use

Suggest which breed a cat or dog in a photo, short clip or webcam frame looks most like, with a calibrated confidence, for a personal, local-only app. It answers "domestic cat, no clear breed" for ordinary cats and "not sure" when a photo doesn't look like anything it was trained on.

**Not for:** proving pedigree, pricing or selling animals, health or behaviour judgements, or anything about people (people in frames are blurred and never classified).

## How it works

RF-DETR finds each animal; the crop goes through a DINOv2 backbone (frozen or with its last blocks fine-tuned); a per-species head gives breed probabilities, temperature-scaled on a calibration split. A kNN distance to the training embeddings flags photos unlike anything seen. A decision policy, tuned on held-out data, turns that into accept, no clear breed, or review.

## This machine

No trained run was found when this card was generated, so it has no numbers. Run `make train` (and `make register promote`), then `make cards`.

## Limitations

- Mixed-breed dogs get the breed they look most like; that is a resemblance, not ancestry.
- Look-alike pairs (Ragdoll and Birman, British Shorthair and Russian Blue) are the most common mistakes; the page shows both when the gap is small.
- Webcam frames in poor light score lower than photos; the live page averages over several frames to steady the answer.
- Trained only on openly licensed photos: breeds with few such photos are less reliable.
