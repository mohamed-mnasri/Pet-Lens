## Known gaps and biases

- Breeds with few openly licensed photos (several Commons categories are nearly empty) have thin training data and wider error bars.
- Commons and Oxford photos are mostly well lit and posed; webcam frames are darker and blurrier, which is why training adds webcam-style degradation.
- "Looks like" is a visual judgement. A label says which breed a photo resembles, not the animal's pedigree.
